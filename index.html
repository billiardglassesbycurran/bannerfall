Goal (Skirmish): peasants gather food (berry bushes, farms), wood (trees) and gold (mines) and carry it to your Town Hall or a Storehouse. Build Houses for more population, a Barracks for soldiers and a Stable for knights. Destroy the enemy Town Hall.

Online: pick Online. One player presses Host a game and reads out the 5-letter code; the other types it and presses Join. Both need this same version (top-right). The host's map is used.

Phone: drag to pan, pinch to zoom, twist to rotate. Tap a unit to select it; double-tap for all of that type; hold then drag to box-select. With units selected, tap the ground to move, an enemy to attack, a tree/bush/mine to gather. The command panel (bottom right) builds and trains. Tap the minimap to jump.

PC / Mac: WASD/arrows pan, Q/E or the ⟲ ⟳ buttons rotate, Option/Alt-drag or right-drag rotates and tilts, wheel or pinch zooms, two-finger sideways swipe rotates, Home resets the view. Left-click select, drag to box, Shift adds, double-click selects a type. Right-click to order. Command panel keys: Z X C V B N M. I = idle peasant, Ctrl+A = army, Tab = play as the selected unit, H = play as your Commander, P = pause.

Riding: left thumb moves, right thumb looks, Strike attacks, Follow me makes nearby troops follow you. PC/Mac: drag the mouse to look around, W/S or ↑/↓ walk, A/D sidestep, ←/→ or Q/E turn, click or Space strikes, F follow, V switches 1st/3rd, walk through a door to go into a building (and out again); G boards a ship / steps off, M mounts or dismounts the Commander's horse, Tab returns; double-click for full mouse-look (Esc releases).

Civilizations: each people has bonuses, weaknesses and one unique unit trained at the Barracks, Archery Range or Stable.
