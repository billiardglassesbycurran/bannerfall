⚔ Begin

Goal: gather food, wood and gold, grow your town, and burn down the Warlord's Town Hall. Peasants pick berries, farm, chop and mine, and drop off at the Town Hall or a Storehouse. Houses raise your population. Research the Feudal Age and then the Kingdom Age at the Town Hall to unlock new units, buildings and upgrades.

Command card: the grid of icons at the bottom. Units get Move, Stop, Hold, Patrol and Attack (pick the command, then the target). Peasants also get Build and Build Military. Buildings train and research there; tap a queued icon in the info panel to cancel it. Hover (PC) or long-press (phone) any icon for its cost and details.

Phone: drag to pan, pinch to zoom, twist two fingers to rotate. Tap a unit to select; double-tap for all of that type; long-press then drag to box-select. With units selected, tap the ground to move, an enemy to attack, a resource to gather, a farm to farm it. Tap the minimap to jump. Riding: left thumb moves, right thumb looks; Strike, Block, Charge and Follow are on the right.

PC (Grid hotkeys, default): command card keys are Q W E R T / A S D F G, laid out like the grid. Arrow keys or the screen edge pan, [ and ] rotate, the wheel zooms. Left-click selects, Shift adds, Ctrl or double-click picks all of a type, drag to box-select. Right-click orders; Shift+right-click queues waypoints. Ctrl+1-9 makes a group, 1-9 recalls it. F1 or period finds idle peasants, F2 or ` selects the army, H leads, Tab rides the selected unit, Space jumps to the last alert, P pauses. Classic hotkeys (Settings) bring back WASD panning with Z X C V B / N M , . / for the card.

PC, riding: click to capture the mouse. WASD moves, the mouse aims, left button strikes, right button or Shift blocks, E charges, F rallies, V switches view, Tab leaves. Look inversion (horizontal and vertical) is in Settings.

Controller: left stick pans, right stick rotates and zooms, A selects or confirms a target, X orders, B backs out, Y selects the army, RT leads, the D-pad presses card buttons. Riding: RT strike, LB block, X charge, Y follow, RB view, B leave.
