# Bannerfall

A medieval real-time strategy game in the browser. Build a town, raise an army and command it from above — then
drop into the battle in 1st or 3rd person as your Commander or any soldier.

**Play:** https://billiardglassesbycurran.github.io/bannerfall/ (phone or PC; turn your phone sideways)

## Civilizations
Franks, Romans, Chinese, Norse and Mongols — each with its own look, bonuses, weaknesses and a unique unit.

## Game modes
- **Skirmish** — gather food, wood and gold, build up, and destroy the enemy Town Hall.
- **Battle** — army against army, no building.
- **Survival** — hold your town against 10 raiding waves.

## Controls

| | Phone | PC |
|---|---|---|
| Camera | drag / pinch / twist | WASD or arrows, wheel, Q/E or right-drag |
| Select | tap · double-tap = all of a type · hold then drag = box | click · drag box · Shift adds · Ctrl+A army |
| Order | tap ground / enemy / tree / bush / mine | right-click |
| Build & train | command panel (bottom right) | command panel or keys Z X C V B N M |
| Ships | build a Dock on the river bank; tap your transport with troops selected to board, then Unload | same, right-click the transport |
| Ride a unit | **Ride** (selected) · **Lead** (hero) | Tab · H |
| Riding | left thumb moves, right thumb looks, **Strike**, **Follow me** | click to aim, WASD, click strike, F follow, V view, Tab back |

The original prototype is in `prototype/` and playable at `/prototype/Bannerfall-prototype.html`.
