# Bannerfall

A medieval RTS in the browser. Command your army from above — then drop into the battle in 1st or 3rd person
as your commander or any soldier.

**Play:** https://billiardglassesbycurran.github.io/bannerfall/ (phone or PC; turn your phone sideways)

## Controls

| | Phone | PC |
|---|---|---|
| Pan / zoom / rotate | drag / pinch / twist | WASD or arrows / wheel / Q-E or right-drag |
| Select | tap a unit · double-tap = all of that type · hold then drag = box | click · drag box · Shift adds · Ctrl+A all |
| Move | tap the ground | right-click the ground |
| Ride a unit | **Ride** button (selected unit) · **Lead** (hero) | Tab (selected) · H (hero) |
| In 3rd person | left thumb moves, right thumb looks | click to aim, WASD, Shift sprint, V 1st/3rd, Tab back |

The original prototype is in `prototype/` and playable at `/prototype/Bannerfall-prototype.html`.
